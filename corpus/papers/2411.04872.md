## Abstract

We introduce FrontierMath, a benchmark of hundreds of original, exceptionally challenging mathematics problems crafted and vetted by expert mathematicians. The questions cover most major branches of modern mathematics -- from computationally intensive problems in number theory and real analysis to abstract questions in algebraic geometry and category theory. Solving a typical problem requires multiple hours of effort from a researcher in the relevant branch of mathematics, and for the upper end questions, multiple days. FrontierMath uses new, unpublished problems and automated verification to reliably evaluate models while minimizing risk of data contamination. Current state-of-the-art AI models solve under 2% of problems, revealing a vast gap between AI capabilities and the prowess of the mathematical community. As AI systems advance toward expert-level mathematical abilities, FrontierMath offers a rigorous testbed that quantifies their progress.

## Why it matters

- FrontierMath introduces a benchmark of hundreds of original, expert-crafted mathematics problems spanning most major branches of modern mathematics, including number theory, real analysis, algebraic geometry, and category theory.
- Problems are designed to require hours to days of effort from professional mathematicians, making the benchmark significantly harder than existing AI math benchmarks.
- The use of new, unpublished problems with automated verification minimizes data contamination risk, ensuring reliable evaluation of model capabilities.
- Current state-of-the-art AI models solve under 2% of problems, exposing a large gap between AI and human expert-level mathematical reasoning and providing a rigorous long-term testbed for measuring progress.
