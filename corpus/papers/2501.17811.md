## Abstract

In this work, we introduce Janus-Pro, an advanced version of the previous work Janus. Specifically, Janus-Pro incorporates (1) an optimized training strategy, (2) expanded training data, and (3) scaling to larger model size. With these improvements, Janus-Pro achieves significant advancements in both multimodal understanding and text-to-image instruction-following capabilities, while also enhancing the stability of text-to-image generation. We hope this work will inspire further exploration in the field. Code and models are publicly available.

## Why it matters

- Janus-Pro unifies multimodal understanding and image generation in a single model, advancing the state of joint vision-language architectures.
- The work demonstrates that scaling both data and model size leads to significant improvements in multimodal understanding benchmarks and text-to-image instruction-following.
- Text-to-image generation stability is notably enhanced, addressing a common practical challenge in generative multimodal systems.
- Public release of code and models enables the broader research community to build on these advances.
