## Abstract

In order to streamline the fine-tuning of foundation models, Low-Rank Adapters (LoRAs) have been substantially adopted across various fields, including instruction tuning and domain adaptation. The underlying concept of LoRA involves decomposing a full-rank matrix into the product of two lower-rank matrices, which reduces storage consumption and accelerates the training process. Furthermore, to address the limited expressive capacity of LoRA, the Mixture-of-Expert (MoE) has been introduced for incorporating multiple LoRA adapters. The integration of LoRA experts leads to a visible improvement across several downstream scenes. However, the mixture of LoRAs (MoE-LoRA) still exhibits its low robustness during tuning and inferring. Inspired by the Riemannian Preconditioners which train LoRA as a sub-space projector, we propose a new training strategy for MoE-LoRA, to stabilize and boost its feature learning procedure by multi-space projections. Examinations on SGD and AdamW optimizers demonstrate the effectiveness of our methodology.

## Why it matters

- Identifies and addresses a key weakness of existing MoE-LoRA approaches: low robustness during both fine-tuning and inference.
- Proposes a novel training strategy inspired by Riemannian Preconditioners, treating LoRA adapters as sub-space projectors to enable multi-space projections across multiple LoRA experts.
- The approach stabilizes and boosts feature learning in MoE-LoRA, demonstrated to work with both SGD and AdamW optimizers.
- Advances parameter-efficient fine-tuning (PEFT) by combining the expressiveness benefits of Mixture-of-Experts with improved training stability for foundation model adaptation.
