## Summary

SmolLM3 is a fully open 3B language model trained on 11.2 trillion tokens that outperforms Llama-3.2-3B and Qwen2.5-3B while competing with 4B-parameter alternatives. The model is designed for efficiency and deploys on-device, while packing several advanced capabilities: dual-mode reasoning via `/think` and `/no_think` toggles, multilingual support across six European languages, and a long context window of up to 128k tokens enabled by NoPE (No Positional Encoding) and YaRN techniques.

Architectural highlights include Grouped Query Attention (GQA) with 4 groups, selective NoPE layer placement, and intra-document masking during training. Pretraining follows a three-stage curriculum that progressively increases math and code data density — from 85% web/12% code/3% math in Stage 1, to a heavily upsampled 63% web/24% code/13% math mix in Stage 3 — covering 11.1 trillion tokens total.

Post-training combines supervised fine-tuning on 1.8 billion tokens (split between reasoning and non-reasoning data, with synthetic reasoning traces generated by Qwen3-32B) followed by Anchored Preference Optimization (APO) for alignment. A model merging step was required to restore long-context capability after APO training caused degradation.

Benchmarks show strong results across knowledge, reasoning, math, and coding tasks. Extended thinking mode delivers dramatic gains: AIME 2025 accuracy rises from 9.3% to 36.7%, and LiveCodeBench from 15.2% to 30.0%, placing SmolLM3 at the efficiency-performance Pareto frontier for sub-4B models.

## Key points

- 3B model trained on 11.2T tokens outperforms Llama-3.2-3B and Qwen2.5-3B, competing with 4B models
- Dual-mode reasoning (`/think`/`/no_think`), 128k context via NoPE+YaRN, and six-language multilingual support
- Three-stage pretraining curriculum with increasing math/code density and intra-document masking
- Post-training uses SFT on synthetic reasoning data (Qwen3-32B-generated) plus Anchored Preference Optimization (APO)
- Model merging recovers long-context capability lost during APO; extended thinking nearly quadruples AIME 2025 accuracy
