## Summary

Google DeepMind announced that an advanced version of Gemini incorporating Deep Think technology achieved gold-medal performance at the 2025 International Mathematical Olympiad (IMO), solving five of six problems for 35 points. This marks a decisive step beyond 2024's silver-medal result, which required domain-specific language translation and two to three days of computation.

The 2025 system operates end-to-end in natural language, producing rigorous mathematical proofs directly from official problem descriptions and completing its work within the standard 4.5-hour competition window. It employs parallel thinking techniques that allow simultaneous exploration of multiple solution approaches. The model was trained with novel reinforcement learning methods, access to curated mathematics solutions, and strategic IMO problem-solving guidance.

IMO President Gregor Dolinar noted that the solutions were "clear, precise and most of them easy to follow." Unlike previous AI approaches that relied on formal theorem-proving languages, the system reasons and communicates entirely in natural language, making its proofs human-readable. Google DeepMind also continues advancing formal verification systems such as AlphaGeometry and AlphaProof, and envisions future hybrid agents that integrate natural-language and formal-proof capabilities as complementary tools for advancing human mathematical knowledge.

## Key points

- Gemini with Deep Think solved 5 of 6 IMO 2025 problems for 35 points, reaching gold-medal standard.
- The system works end-to-end in natural language within the official 4.5-hour time limit, requiring no domain-specific language translation.
- Parallel thinking allows simultaneous exploration of multiple reasoning paths, trained via novel reinforcement learning on curated math data.
- Solutions were evaluated as clear and precise by IMO leadership, and DeepMind envisions hybrid formal+natural-language agents for future mathematical discovery.
